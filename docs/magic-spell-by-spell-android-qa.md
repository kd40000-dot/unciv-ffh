## Mandatory shared regressions

- [ ] Fire Node→Body Node retune clears Fire Mana and adds Body Mana (no duplication).
- [ ] A promotion cannot be learned without required Mana and cannot be cast when disabled by Mana loss.
- [ ] Adepts cannot choose II/III rank or cast higher-rank spells; Mages/Wizards can choose II but not III; Archmage can choose III (with experience).
- [ ] New summoned units do not unexpectedly become buildable/purchasable and cannot be exploited to infinitely duplicate valuable effects.
- [ ] Promotion names/statuses and all unit action references appear correctly in Civilopedia and unit panel.
- [ ] Unit action consumes stated movement, cannot illegally target enemy/foreign land, and does not crash/save-corrupt.
- [ ] No status stacks unexpectedly; recast refreshes expiration; save/reload preserves temporary states.
- [ ] Enemy unit status effects, area damage, AI and summoned lifetimes are reviewed separately: native Unciv proxies lack FFH target semantics.

## Non-sphere EMM spell records

The separate [full 355-entry inventory](emm-full-spell-inventory.md) is authoritative for the 288 non-sphere records. They include equipment drop/take actions, religion/faction spells, world spells, automation and UI actions; **these are NOT implemented by the 63 sphere promotions**. Implement them only when the related EMM units, religions, artifacts, map features and engine mechanics have been ported. Track them as unresolved instead of treating placeholder promotions as functional.
