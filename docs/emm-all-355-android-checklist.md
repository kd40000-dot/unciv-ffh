# Full EMM spell-by-spell Android acceptance checklist

This checklist covers every source SpellInfo, **including deferred abilities**. A checkbox must remain unchecked until the exact source spell is represented by a runnable native action/proxy AND tested in game. An approximation must retain its difference note. Ruleset CI alone does not check a box.

For every entry: verify unlock and target restrictions, caster survival/cost, effect and duration, persistence after save/load, and repeat casting. Deferred items must not be represented as implemented.
