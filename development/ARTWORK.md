# Artwork credits and provenance

**This project's first graphics pack is a temporary, noncanonical illustrated substitute, not copied leader models from Fall from Heaven II or ExtraModMod.**

- Art source: **The Battle for Wesnoth**, community artists and contributors, [wesnoth/wesnoth](https://github.com/wesnoth/wesnoth)
- Specific upstream revision is recorded in [sources.json](art/sources.json), along with the individual source path of each portrait and unit sprite.
- Upstream art is distributed under the GNU GPL (see [WESNOTH-COPYING](licenses/WESNOTH-COPYING)). The generated scaled/cropped images retain that license and attribution.
- Original ExtraModMod/Fall from Heaven II 3D animation files are not imported: Unciv's map units use 2D sprites rather than the 3D Civ IV model format.
- The images named Valledia, Sabathiel, Arendel, and Jonas are **visual stand-ins** and not portraits of those canonical characters.

Production images are generated by [build_art.py](tools/build_art.py) and packed into Unciv atlases by the official desktop `mod-ci` tool. This is mandatory for Android display.
