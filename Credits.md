# Credits

This is an independent fan adaptation of ExtraModMod Continued to Unciv.

- **ExtraModMod Continued**: https://github.com/bewlay/extramodmod
- **Fall from Heaven II**: original mod and world from Derek "Kael" Paxton and collaborators.
- **Unciv**: https://github.com/yairm210/Unciv
- **Current placeholder graphics**: The Battle for Wesnoth art contributors. The generated graphics are resized/cropped adaptations of GPL artwork, not the original FFH2 characters or 3D models. The specific file paths and pinned source revision are in [development/art/sources.json](development/art/sources.json). The upstream GPL license text is included at [development/licenses/WESNOTH-COPYING](development/licenses/WESNOTH-COPYING).

Asset rendering and atlas-packing scripts are in `development/tools/`.
